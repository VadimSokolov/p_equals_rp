# Chapter 16. Pseudorandomness and Hitting Sets

This chapter gives proofs of classical results on randomized classes and describes the derandomization machinery. It proves amplification, Adleman's theorem (RP and BPP in P/poly), the Sipser, Gács and Lautemann theorem (BPP in the second level of the polynomial hierarchy, by translates of the acceptance set), and the Valiant Vazirani isolation lemma with its consequence that a polynomial algorithm for unique satisfiability gives NP = RP. It describes the Nisan and Wigderson pseudorandom generator from a hard function and a combinatorial design, hardness amplification, hitting set generators for RP, and the Kabanets and Impagliazzo theorem that derandomizing polynomial identity testing implies a circuit lower bound. After each result it gives a reading in the vertical coordinates of the book, where the level set of an RP algorithm is the object a hitting set must hit. The chapter restates known theorems and proves no new result; it does not prove P = RP.
