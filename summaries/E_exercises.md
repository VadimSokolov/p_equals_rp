Exercises arranged by the four parts of the book, from routine verifications to small research projects; those marked with an asterisk are, as far as the authors know, open as stated. Part IV exercises include writing the quantile function of an RP algorithm after amplification, proving the search to decision reduction for RP, and showing that polynomial mixing of the level set chain on every partial board gives NP = RP.
