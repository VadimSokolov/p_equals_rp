# Chapter 4. Nondeterminism and the Classes P and NP

This chapter defines NP in three equivalent ways, by certificates, by nondeterministic Turing machines and by projection of a polynomial time relation, and develops the structure around the class: coNP, the intersection NP and coNP, polynomial time (Karp) and Turing (Cook) reductions, Ladner's theorem on NP intermediate problems, oracle machines and the polynomial hierarchy, and the equivalence of search and decision through self reducibility. The queens completion problem is the running example: it is formalised as a language with a partial placement as input and a full placement as certificate, it is in NP, and a short refutation certificate would give NP = coNP. The chapter links self reducibility to the Jerrum Valiant Vazirani correspondence between approximate counting and sampling that the Monte Carlo program later uses. It states known results and presents no new theorem.
