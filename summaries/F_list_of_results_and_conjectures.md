A one line index, with chapter references, of what the book proves, computes, cites and conjectures. Proved: the Lorenz identity, the quantile form of RP and its hitting set restatement (not a proof of P = RP), the main equivalence and the other theorems. Computed: density of states, giant component to N = 13 and N = 17, connectivity of the second level set, shattering under random conditioning, spectral gaps, depth growth, finite size correction. Cited: the theorems of others used. Conjectured: mixing and expansion on unconditioned boards, torpidity on hard boards, shattering depth, the rate function, no polynomial correction, a completion threshold.
