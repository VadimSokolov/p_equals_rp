What is the best case that NP = RP, and does anything measured in the book contradict it? This chapter states the opposing hypothesis as its defenders would, and says the authors do not hold it. The argument from the data lists the polynomial looking quantities: bottom rung autocorrelation time growing like N^1.8 to N = 32, relaxation time of the swap chain growing like N^3 to N = 12, a giant component of the bottom level set, near solution paths of bounded length. The argument from structure says the queens have no random constraints, and compares the swap chain with the Jerrum, Sinclair and Vigoda proof for the permanent. The argument from complexity theory says that NP different from RP is a belief, not a theorem, and recalls the barriers (relativization, natural proofs, algebrization). The argument from the main equivalence says NP = RP needs only one mixing sampler. The chapter ends with the experiments the defenders would run (shattering depth of a reduction board) and the authors' reply. No theorem is proved.

## From the literature
