# Chapter 26. Random Walks on the Symmetric Group

The swap chain on a level set of the queens is the random transposition walk on the symmetric group restricted to a subset, and this chapter presents the theory of the unrestricted walk and asks what survives the restriction. It states the exact spectrum from the representation theory of the symmetric group, the cutoff of the random transposition walk at (1/2) N log N steps (Diaconis and Shahshahani), the comparison techniques of Diaconis and Saloff Coste for transferring spectral gap bounds between reversible chains, and Fourier analysis of the energy function on permutations. It explains that the spectrum is lost on a restricted subset but comparison methods carry over and give a possible route to mixing bounds on the giant component of a level set, and discusses cutoff for the level sets. It is the group theoretic counterpart of the isoperimetric route of the volume problem. No new theorem is stated.
