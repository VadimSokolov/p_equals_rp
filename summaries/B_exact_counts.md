A table of the number Q(N) of solutions of the N queens problem for 4 at most N at most 27, with log base ten of Q(N)/N!, taken from the Online Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences entry A000170 (exhaustive computation by others). The last column falls by about 0.41 per unit of N beyond N about 15, consistent with Simkin's constant 0.942.

## From the literature

Exact enumeration of queens solutions up to N = 27 (the OEIS A000170 tabulation, computations by others, including Preusser and Engelhardt for N = 27 according to the chronology); Simkin's constant (Simkin 2023).
