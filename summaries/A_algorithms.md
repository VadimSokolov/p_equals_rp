Pseudocode for three algorithms used in the book: nested sampling with n live points, split sampling with a discrete ladder (a quantile fraction rho and sample size M per level, giving the product estimator of N(0)), and Wang and Landau flat histogram sampling for the density of states. For the queens the conditioned draw in each is a Markov chain on permutations restricted to a level set.

## From the literature

Nested sampling (Skilling 2006); Wang and Landau (2001); split sampling (Birge, Chang and Polson 2013 as given in the chronology).
